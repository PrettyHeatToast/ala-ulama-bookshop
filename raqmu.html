Souvenirs of Petra · field notes · not for publication

14 March 1998

Stood today where Roberts stood for plate 92. His drawing shows a small square niche high in the rock, left of the third tomb, with a stone in it. Two eyes cut in the stone. The niche is there. It is empty.

2 April 1998

Compared all his Petra plates in the library in Amman. The stone is in five of them. Never in the same niche twice. In the Haghe prints it has been taken out. Every one, except a proof I have only seen in a catalogue, printed in Tournai.

20 October 2009

Found it. In the Siq, a small niche above the old water channel. It was there in the morning and not at noon, and there again at four. I photographed it. That is card 712U.

6 March 2016

I do not sell it. I have never sold it. But when I hold it I remember my mother's sister, whose name nobody in the family could say anymore. I could say it. I could say it for three days, and then I could not remember the way to my own shop.

4 April 2016

A family from Belgium. Kind people. The box was on the counter. I was making tea.

11 February 2017

Withdrew the variants it is in, one by one, in the order I first saw it. I kept one in print, the drawing by Roberts, and took the stone out of it with a brush. Anyone who has a set can read the name back. That is enough of a door.

14 August 2018

No letters from Gent for months. I am closing the site. The faces in Petra were cut for people who were forgotten, so that something would stay standing. I understand that now. I do not know who it is standing for in Gent.

 

The site was dead and somebody has woken it. If you are reading this in Gent: it likes faces in stone, above doors, where people walk past without looking up. Look up.

M.
