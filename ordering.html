Ordering

Orders are suspended until further notice.

When orders resume, they can be placed by post or by e-mail. Please give the product numbers, quantities and your full postal address. We will reply with the total including postage.

Shipping

  • Jordan: 3 to 5 days.
  • Europe: 2 to 4 weeks by registered post.
  • Other countries: please ask.

What we do not send

We sell paper only. We do not sell or ship antiquities, stones or carved objects of any kind, and we never have.

If an item came to you in one of our boxes and it is not paper, it did not come from us. Please do not open the box again. Please write to us.
