Welcome to Ala Ulama Bookshop

Ala Ulama Bookshop publishes and distributes books, maps and postcards for travellers to Petra and the south of Jordan.

We opened our first counter at the Visitor Centre road in Wadi Musa in 1981. Since then we have specialised in guidebooks and photographic souvenirs of the Rose City. All postcards in our Souvenirs of Petra series are photographed by our own house, standing where the painter David Roberts stood in March 1839.
